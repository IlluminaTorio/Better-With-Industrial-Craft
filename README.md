# Better With Industrial Craft

A port of IndustrialCraft² v1.00 (originally for Minecraft Beta 1.7.3) to Better Than Adventure 8.0.1, with BTA-specific additions.

## Ported from the original

- Ores (copper, tin, uranium) and rubber trees with world generation
- Full power grid: cables, insulation, energy loss, breakdowns, electric shock
- Machines: iron/electric furnace, macerator, extractor, compressor, canner, recycler, electrolyzer, induction furnace, mass fabricator, magnetizer
- Generators: coal, geothermal, water mill, solar panel, wind mill, nuclear reactor
- Energy storage and conversion: BatBox, MFE, MFSU, transformers
- Tools and armor: mining drill, chainsaw, mining laser, nano saber, wrench, scanner, bronze/composite/nano/quantum armor, jetpacks

## Additions for BTA

- Ore variants for all BTA stone types (basalt, limestone, granite, permafrost)
- Steel economy: macerator and steel dust recipes integrated with BTA steel
- Industrial diamond is interchangeable with vanilla diamond in BTA recipes
- Bronze wolf armor, bronze door and fence
- Trommel support and IC2 drops in vanilla trommel loot
- Scrap box drops include BTA items
- All recipes registered in the BTA recipe book
- Optional integrations: TMB recipe viewer, BTWaila tooltips, Signal Industries / Catalyst energy converters
- Config options via HalpLibe (world generation, explosions, cable burning)
